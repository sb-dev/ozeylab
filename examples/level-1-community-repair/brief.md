# Plan community repair sessions — exercise brief

Status: synthetic learning input. No market or execution result is established.

A volunteer group wants a reliable monthly repair session for household items. Its goal is useful repairs and an accessible booking process, not profit. The exercise assumes six volunteers with two hours each per month and a £300 setup limit. Venue, insurance, tools, competence, demand and funding are not confirmed. Compare a paper-and-phone process, configured existing booking tools, and a small custom tool. Keeping the current informal process is the baseline. Do not claim that any repair activity or venue is approved.

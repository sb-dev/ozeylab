## Exact prompt

```text
Read examples/RUNNING.md and examples/level-2-childrens-audio/brief.md. Follow their evidence, review and execution rules.

Use case-study-create for example-l2-02. Compare commissions, catalogue licensing and subscriptions as separate operating models. Screen the remaining families and include no new commitment. Identify the buyer, listener, payer, rights transferred or retained, and production work per route. Model production capacity, time to first cash, payment timing, owner hours and finite subscription-retention scenarios. Treat audience growth, licence demand and renewal as untested assumptions. Include the no-deal outcome for a catalogue built before sale. Recommend a route and one alternative with conditions that would reverse the ranking.

Report actual output paths, checks performed, unresolved evidence and the next decision. Distinguish a producer self-check from independent review.
```

## Expected outputs

Paths below are relative to `projects/example-l2-02/`.

- case-study.md: rights-aware alternatives and capacity limits
- wiki/: source-linked audience, buyer and production findings
- finance/: dated cash, production hours and finite retention scenarios

## Check the result

- Listener, purchasing decision-maker and payer are distinguished.
- The same catalogue rights are not promised exclusively to multiple buyers.
- Forecast production volume fits writing, editing and review capacity.

Apply the shared verification rules as well. A structural pass does not establish demand, correct judgement or a successful launch.

## Next decision

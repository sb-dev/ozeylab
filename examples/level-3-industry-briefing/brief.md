# Bootstrap a paid industry briefing — exercise brief

Status: synthetic learning input. No market or execution result is established.

Proposed selected route: a weekly briefing for a clearly named professional audience. Use independent coffee-shop operators as the exercise audience. Each issue covers three dated developments, their operating implications and source links. Claims require inspected primary evidence. An issue may contain fewer items when evidence is insufficient. The starting repository includes editorial rules, source and rights records, one sample issue and a local HTML export. No subscriber list, paid demand or permission to send an issue is supplied.

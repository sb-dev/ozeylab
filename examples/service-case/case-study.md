# Synthetic service case

This illustrates structure and arithmetic only. No live demand or price is verified.

## Conditional recommendation

At £50 per owner hour and a 36-month horizon, the service scenario has the greatest
calculated result. A package sale is preferable at 12 months or at £80 per owner hour
under these inputs. Neither recommendation is supported by actual market evidence.

## Starting position and coverage

Assume a wholly owned domain with an available £2,000 offer for this illustration.
Baseline: sale net of 10% fees. All other assumptions are invented.

| Family | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Assets and rights | Domain sale and a transferable brand/site package |
| Delivered services | Retained podcast production service |
| Software and tools | Research candidate; no tested user workflow or distribution advantage |
| Information and access | Research candidate; no audience evidence |
| Matching and transactions | Research candidate; no agency acceptance or acquisition evidence |
| Goods and fulfilment | No physical product or supplier advantage identified in this example |

## Package option

Buyer: an existing production agency. Deliverable: editable brand assets, website
source, copy and handover. Development: package assets, with commissioned design as
an alternative. Marketing: direct agency conversations; a marketplace listing is the
alternative. Self-fund £1,600 and £10/month holding costs. Transfer the agreed assets.
A £12,000 sale in month 6 with 10% fees is conditional. Also model no sale.
Repository: asset inventory, rights, website source and transfer checks.
Launch: find a buyer, agree terms, receive payment and transfer usable assets.

## Service option

Buyer: a small organisation commissioning a podcast. Offer: an assumed £600/month
service with £150/client-month delivery costs. Owner retains the operation.
Acquisition: direct sales, with agency referrals as the alternative; model £150/new
customer plus owner selling time. Human review remains part of delivery.
Development: configure existing recording/editing tools; compare commissioning extra
capacity before custom software. Costs include £1,200 setup and £100/month fixed costs.
Repository: offer, intake, quotation, delivery procedure, sample output and quality checks.
Launch: win and deliver the first paid service. Check revisions, capacity and retention.

## Financial comparison

See the reproducible models and results in finance/. GBP, before tax, full ownership.
90/365/1095-day horizons use the explicit day schedule. Models distinguish conditional
sale, no sale and retained operation. At 36 months: domain £1,800 cash; package £9,140;
unsold package −£1,960; service £70,800. Owner hours: 2, 80, 80 and 864 respectively.
These are conditional calculations, not expected returns.

## Threshold and next evidence

The package needs more than £8,177.78 gross sale proceeds at month 6 to beat the
domain sale at £50/hour. Test buyer willingness, actual build effort and transfer scope.
The service needs observed delivery costs, paying acquisition and retention evidence.
No route is selected for execution by this example.

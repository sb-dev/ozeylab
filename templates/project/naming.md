# Naming

## Brief

Record what needs naming, operating model, audience, positioning, languages,
markets, exclusions, accepted names, domain extensions and cost limits.

## Candidates

Compare distinct naming approaches, meaning, spelling, pronunciation,
memorability, distinctiveness, expansion scope and scoped conflict searches.
Preserve an accepted name unless a rename is requested.

## Domain evidence

Link dated evidence in sources/domains/. Keep availability, ownership,
premium pricing and resale separate. No checks have been performed.

## Recommendation

State the preferred name and a credible alternative. Record acceptance in
project.md and decisions.md, with evidence references.
